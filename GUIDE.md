# 👻 GhostFi User Guide: How to Bank via Chat

Welcome to GhostFi. This guide will help you manage your private finances entirely through WhatsApp or USSD.

## 1. Getting Started
To begin, simply send a message like "Hello" to our WhatsApp bot or dial our USSD code.
*   **WhatsApp**: Your wallet is automatically created and tied to your phone number.
*   **Security**: GhostFi uses a server-side custodial wallet. Never share your phone access with anyone.

## 2. Funding Your Account (`fund`)
You don't need an existing crypto wallet to use GhostFi.
1.  Text `fund 50` (to add 50 USDC).
2.  Click the secure checkout link provided by the bot.
3.  Pay via Credit Card or Bank Transfer.
4.  **Wait**: Within 1-2 minutes, you will receive your USDC and a small "Gas Drip" of SOL to cover network fees.

## 3. Going Private (`shield`)
By default, transactions on Solana are public. To make your balance invisible:
1.  Text `shield 50`.
2.  Your funds are moved into an **Encrypted On-Chain Balance**.
3.  Only you can see this balance using your private keys (or the bot).

## 4. Spending with your Virtual Card (`card`)
You can spend your private balance at any merchant that accepts Visa.
1.  **Issue Card**: Visit the GhostFi web dashboard to issue your first card.
2.  **Check Balance**: Text `card` to see your current spending limit.
3.  **Load Funds**: Text `topup card 25`. This unshields 25 USDC from your private balance and loads it onto your Visa card instantly.
4.  **View Details**: Text `reveal card` to get your card number, CVV, and Expiry via a secure, self-deleting message.

## 5. Sending Money Privately (`send`)
Send money to anyone with a phone number without leaving a trace on the public explorer.
1.  Text `send 20 +1234567890`.
2.  The money is sent via the **GhostFi Mixer**.
3.  The recipient will receive a notification to claim their private funds.

# ⚖️ GhostFi Terms of Service (Simplified)

### 1. Privacy & Compliance
GhostFi is a privacy-first platform. While your balances are encrypted on-chain, you are responsible for following your local laws.
*   **Viewing Keys**: If you are asked for proof of funds by a legal authority, you can export your **Viewing Key** from our web dashboard to provide a read-only view of your history.

### 2. KYC (Know Your Customer)
To use the Virtual Card for large purchases, you must complete a one-time identity verification as required by our banking partners (Lithic).

### 3. Custodianship
The Bot wallet is custodial. GhostFi manages the encryption keys on your behalf to enable "Just Chatting" functionality. For 100% self-sovereignty, use our Web Dashboard with your own Phantom or Solflare wallet.

### 4. Fees
*   **Network Fees**: Every transaction on Solana costs a tiny bit of SOL. We provide an initial "Gas Drip," but you must ensure you have enough SOL for frequent transactions.
*   **Swap Fees**: Token swaps (USDC -> PUSD) are handled by Jupiter and involve standard market slippage.
